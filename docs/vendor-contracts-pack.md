# Vendor Contract Pack — Getting the Agreements & Keeping Control

Prepared 2026-08-13. Goal: obtain each vendor's reseller/white-label agreement
and sign only on terms where **we set retail pricing, we bill, we own the
customer relationship, and we can leave without losing the book of business.**

## The reality on "exact copies"

Reseller and white-label agreements are **not public documents**. Each vendor
issues theirs after a partnership inquiry, typically with an NDA. What IS
public today:

| Vendor | Public today | How to get the real contract |
|---|---|---|
| Preferred Patron | [Terms of Service](https://www.preferredpatron.com/terms-of-service.html) and [reseller program overview](https://www.preferredpatron.com/reseller-loyalty-programs) | Email/call via the reseller page — Email #1 below |
| White Label Loyalty | [Standard Terms of Service](https://whitelabel-loyalty.com/terms-of-service/) (customer terms, not partner terms) | Book a demo / partnerships contact — Email #2 below |
| Loyalty Partners Group | Marketing site only ([loyaltypartnersgroup.com](https://loyaltypartnersgroup.com/)) | Partner inquiry — Email #3 below |
| Access Development (alt.) | Marketing site only | Partner inquiry (same shape as #3) |

**Known terms already visible in White Label Loyalty's public ToS — flag for
negotiation before signing anything:**

1. **Automatic 10% price increase** at the start of each new service term
   unless the Service Order says otherwise. → Our redline: fixed pricing for
   the initial term + renewal increases capped at CPI or a stated %.
2. **90-day termination notice**, and on termination all charges through the
   end of the term **accelerate and become due immediately**. → Our redline:
   shorter notice, no acceleration, and a wind-down license so client
   programs keep running during migration.
3. Customer **retains ownership of customer data** (good — keep that, and
   extend it explicitly to member-level data across all client programs).

>
> Hi —
>
> I run a loyalty-program agency serving multi-location and multifamily
> clients, with an active pipeline in the $200K–$300K/yr program range. I want
> to move forward under your white-label reseller program — the model where we
> price, sell, bill, and support under our own brand.
>
> Please send:
> 1. Your standard white-label reseller agreement (full text) for our
>    attorney's review;
> 2. Wholesale pricing schedule by plan tier, kiosk hardware, and card orders;
> 3. Confirmation that we set retail pricing with no caps or MAP restrictions;
> 4. Confirmation that client contracts and billing run on our paper, and what
>    happens to those client accounts if either side terminates;
> 5. Data-ownership terms for member-level data in programs we deploy;
> 6. Any NDA you need signed first — send it with the rest.
>
> We're moving quickly and intend to select platforms within 30 days.

## Term-sheet checklist — every vendor contract must clear these

Give this to the attorney as the redline standard. **No-go items in bold.**

**Pricing & billing control**
- [ ] **We set retail pricing; no caps, floors, or MAP clauses**
- [ ] **All client billing on our paper; vendor invoices only us (wholesale)**
- [ ] Wholesale pricing locked for initial term; renewal increases capped (CPI or stated %)
- [ ] No per-transaction/redemption fees that scale against us (or fully disclosed schedule)
- [ ] Volume discounts defined in writing as our client count grows

**Customer ownership**
- [ ] **Clients are OUR customers: contract, billing, support front door, brand**
- [ ] **Non-circumvention: vendor may not solicit, poach, or directly contract with our clients — during the term and 2–3 years after**
- [ ] Vendor identity concealed from end clients (true white label), or disclosed only as our subcontractor
- [ ] All client-facing assets (portals, apps, cards, emails) carry our/our client's brand only

**Data**
- [ ] **We/our clients own all program data and member-level data**
- [ ] Full export at any time, standard formats (CSV/JSON/API), no export fees
- [ ] Vendor may not market to, or monetize, our members
- [ ] DPA in place; US privacy compliance (state laws), PCI where cards touch

**Exit & continuity**
- [ ] **On termination: wind-down period (6–12 mo) where client programs keep running while we migrate**
- [ ] **No acceleration of remaining-term fees on termination** (WLL's public ToS has this — strike it)
- [ ] Points/liability ledger handed over on exit
- [ ] Termination for convenience with reasonable notice (30–60 days our side)
- [ ] If vendor is acquired or shuts down: source-code or data escrow, or guaranteed migration support

**Protection**
- [ ] SLAs with uptime %, support response times, and credits
- [ ] Liability cap mutual and not absurdly low (target ≥ 12 mo of fees)
- [ ] Indemnity from vendor for IP infringement and data breach
- [ ] No exclusivity forced on us (we run a multi-vendor stack); territory protection for us where available
- [ ] Assignment clause lets US assign the agreement in a sale of our company **without vendor consent or with consent not unreasonably withheld** — critical for exit

**Sales-agent traps to refuse**
- [ ] Not an "agent/referral" structure where the vendor holds the client contract
- [ ] No clause converting our clients to vendor's direct customers on termination
- [ ] No commission-only economics on accounts we source and service
