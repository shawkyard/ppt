**The $20–30K monthly recurring in 6–8 months = one enterprise deal, or one
smaller enterprise deal plus 5–10 mid-market accounts.** It does not require
a miracle; it requires closing deals we already know how to sell.

## Why $200K–$300K/yr is a market-validated price (not our invention)

Enterprise brands already pay this to platform vendors directly:

- **Annex Cloud** deployments run **$50K–$250K+/yr**, floor around $50K/yr.
- **Antavo** total annual cost runs **$24K–$120K/yr**, plus a one-off
  implementation fee (scoped by SOW) and 15–20% annual maintenance.
- **White Label Loyalty** starts around **£1,999/mo (~$2.5K/mo, ~$30K/yr)**
  at the platform floor; complex omnichannel programs are custom-priced well
  above that.

A client paying us $250K/yr is getting platform + strategy + program
management + marketing + integrations — the bundle brands otherwise assemble
from a platform vendor plus an agency plus internal headcount. Our price is
inside the established market band.

## Why this is low-risk (the investor version)

1. **No product-build risk.** We are not spending $120K–$320K and 6–18 months
   building software. Platform COGS is variable and only incurred when a
   client is already paying us 2–4x that amount.
2. **Founder has 11 years selling loyalty programs** and an active pipeline;
   the constraint has been delivery capacity, which this vendor stack solves.
3. **Recurring revenue on our paper.** White-label reseller structure means
   clients contract and pay US. That's what a future acquirer pays a multiple
   for — referral commissions would be worth roughly nothing at exit.
4. **Diversified stack.** Two platform vendors (enterprise + mid-market) plus
   merchant-funded reward content — no single-vendor dependency, and vendor
   contracts negotiated with assignment rights so they transfer in a sale of
   the company.

## Honest caveats (say these before they ask)

- Wholesale partner pricing is **not yet in writing** from any vendor;
  figures above bracket list prices and standard reseller discount ranges.
  Hard quotes expected within 1–2 weeks of tonight.
- White Label Loyalty's standard terms include an automatic 10% renewal
  uplift and a termination-acceleration clause — flagged for negotiation
  before any signature.
- Enterprise sales cycles for $200K+ programs typically run months; the
  6–8-month $20–30K MRR figure leans on pipeline already in motion.
