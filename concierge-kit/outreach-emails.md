# Outreach email scripts

Copy, fill the [brackets], send from your regular Gmail. Personalized one-at-a-time
sends beat BCC blasts every time — but both versions are here.

---

## Email 1 — personalized cold email (use this 90% of the time)

**Subject:** 5 hours a week back — [Business Name]

Hi [First name],

I'm [Your name], a local AI consultant here in [Town]. I help businesses like
yours hand the repetitive stuff — quotes, missed calls, follow-ups, invoicing —
to AI tools that quietly get it done in the background.

[ONE personal line: "Drove past your shop on Main St yesterday" / "Saw your
crew's truck on the Hendersons' roof" / "Loved your Google reviews — 4.9 is no
joke". This line is why the email gets read.]

Simple deal: a free 15-minute assessment, phone or in person. If I can't find
you at least five hours a week worth saving, I'll tell you so, and you'll never
hear from me again.

Worth 15 minutes? Just reply and I'll work around your schedule.

[Your name]
[Phone]
[yoursite.netlify.app]

P.S. Everything I set up lives in **your** accounts, at cost — no markup, no
lock-in, no contracts.

---

## Email 2 — the bump (send 3 days later if no reply)

**Subject:** Re: 5 hours a week back — [Business Name]

Hi [First name] — floating this back up.

One example of what I mean: for most [trades/shops/clinics] the first fix is
missed calls. Every call you can't pick up gets an instant friendly text back
that answers questions and books the job. Owners tell me that alone saves the
first lead they'd have lost that week.

The 15-minute assessment is free either way. Interested?

[Your name] · [Phone]

---

## Email 3 — the goodbye (day 8, last touch; this one gets replies)

**Subject:** Re: 5 hours a week back — [Business Name]

Hi [First name],

I'll take the hint and close the file on this one. If the busy season buries
you and you want those hours back later, my free assessment offer stands —
just reply to this email whenever.

Good luck out there,
[Your name]

## BCC batch version (only when you truly can't personalize)

Send to **yourself in To:**, prospects in **BCC** (never CC — CC exposes
everyone's addresses to each other). Keep batches to 20–30; Gmail caps you
around 500 recipients/day, and big identical blasts trip spam filters fast.

**Subject:** A free offer for [Town] business owners

Hi there,

Quick one from a neighbor: I'm [Your name], a local AI consultant in [Town].
I help small businesses hand their repetitive work — quotes, missed-call
follow-up, invoicing, review replies — to AI tools that quietly get it done.

My standing offer: a free 15-minute assessment. If I can't find you at least
five hours a week worth saving, I'll say so and that's the end of it.

Reply to this email or call/text me at [Phone] and I'll work around your hours.

[Your name]
[yoursite.netlify.app]

If you'd rather not hear from me again, just reply "no thanks" — no hard
feelings, and I'll take you off my list.

---

## Rules that keep you out of trouble

- **BCC, never CC**, for anything with multiple prospects.
- Include your real name, business identity, and a way to opt out (the "no
  thanks" line) — that keeps commercial email on the right side of CAN-SPAM.
- Honor every opt-out immediately: mark them "Not interested" in the tracker.
- Log every send in the prospect tracker the same day, while it's fresh.
