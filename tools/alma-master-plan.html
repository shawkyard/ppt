DELIVERABLE 01

Executive Brand Brief

The business

Prestige Sales Experts is a done-for-you revenue company. Its customer-facing line is Alma Revenue Hunter: one intelligence system + a fleet of specialist agents that finds opportunities, builds the web presence that attracts buyers, and installs the systems that convert them.

What Alma is

One intelligence system — not 100 bots. Studies a business, learns its ideal buyers, detects signals, finds companies & decision-makers, explains why an opportunity exists now, recommends the next action. DFY today, DIY later.

The customer

Owners & revenue leaders of small–mid businesses across ~100 industries who have a website and maybe a CRM, but no fleet actively hunting revenue.

The difference (5-sec thesis)

Your website waits. Alma hunts. Everyone has a website; almost nobody has intelligent agents finding opportunities before competitors see them.

Primary offer: Start a Hunting Expedition (DFY). Secondary: Farm, Grow, and combined Hunt+Farm / full Hunt·Farm·Grow systems.

Agent Catalog & Industry Architecture

Naming: Alma {Mission} Hunter — one family, one OS. Six mission categories, not 100 cards.

Buyer & Lead Hunters

Companies + decision-makers with a reason to buy.

Signal Hunters

Hiring, expansion, project-award, permit, funding, acquisition, vendor-change.

Revenue Recovery Hunters

Unbilled work, inactive customers, missed follow-ups.

Recruiting Hunters

Employers with immediate/emerging staffing demand.

Sourcing Hunters

Scarce equipment, inventory, real-estate, hard-to-find opportunities.

Local Market Hunters

Qualified prospects in a city / region / territory.

Industries: 25 groups, ~100 industries on one data-driven template. 11 launch-ready from existing content — HVAC, Plumbing, Electrical, Roofing, Auto, Legal, Real Estate, Fitness, Construction, Salons/Spas, IT & B2B — then Healthcare, Restaurants, Dental, Financial, Insurance, Property Mgmt, Recruiting.
