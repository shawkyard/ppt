Who you're dealing with

Local, hands-on, and honest about what AI can and can't do

I'm a local consultant — not an agency, not a call center, not a software company. I've spent the last couple of years deep in AI tools, figuring out which ones actually hold up in a real business and which ones are hype.

My rule is simple: I only recommend what I'd run in my own business, I tell you exactly what every tool costs before we start, and everything gets set up in your accounts — so you're never locked in to me.

Fair questions

Is this going to be complicated for my staff?

No — that's the whole point. Most of what I set up runs in the background or inside tools you already use (your phone, your email, your calendar). Training is in plain English, and I don't leave until your team is comfortable.

What do the AI tools themselves cost?

Most small businesses end up between $50 and $200/month in tool subscriptions, depending on what we set up. I tell you the exact number in your Action Plan before you spend anything — and the tools are in your name, at cost. I don't mark them up.

Will AI say something weird to my customers?

We set guardrails: what it's allowed to answer, when it hands off to a human, and the tone it uses. Anything customer-facing gets tested with you before it goes live, and you can review everything it sends.

What if it doesn't save me time?

The assessment is free, and if I can't show you a credible five-plus hours a week, I'll tell you that to your face and we're done — no charge. On builds, we agree on what "working" means up front, and I don't consider the job finished until it does.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. The audit and builds are one-time projects. AI Partner is month to month — cancel any time, and everything keeps working because it's all in your accounts.
