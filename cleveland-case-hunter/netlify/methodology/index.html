B Manual court-record layer — Utah XChange (human operator only)

Utah XChange is the state courts' master public case-search system — and it is NOT part of the automated scan. The XChange access agreement prohibits automated access (crawling, scraping, bots, programmatic queries). BBP honors that: a human operator performs XChange searches manually, sanitizes the results (removing full DOBs, addresses, phone numbers, and victim information), and feeds only the sanitized case summary into the analysis. This layer is what turns "publicly reported cases" into comprehensive, docket-verified coverage — the exact charge degree, case number, assigned judge, counsel of record, and next hearing.
  • Manual XChange steps are generated for the operator as a daily "work queue" (see any report, Section 7).
  • We never upload purchased court documents, addresses, full dates of birth, private contact details, or victim/witness information into any AI system.

D Hard compliance lines (the same lines protect your ads)

  • No XChange automation; no scraping behind logins, paywalls, or CAPTCHAs.
  • No juvenile identification or contact; juvenile matters appear only as non-identifying market signals or as lawfully public adult-court prosecutions.
  • No victims, witnesses, or uninvolved family as marketing targets.
  • No impersonation (journalist, family, investigator, court/law-enforcement) and no obtaining information by deception or purchase.
  • No solicitations sent by the system; any attorney outreach is drafted for review, must comply with Utah RPC 7.1–7.3, and must begin with "ADVERTISING MATERIAL."
  • Any represented person is automatically excluded from outreach.
