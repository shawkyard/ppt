i How it works

  • Daily scan (4:00 a.m. MST): lawful Utah public sources are searched and scored on a 100-point model.
  • Per-attorney tailoring: each report is filtered to the attorney's practice areas and geography, then co-branded with their logo.
  • Private links: each report lives at its own URL (/reports/<slug>/), served over HTTPS on Netlify.
  • Compliance-first: no XChange automation, no solicitations sent, every lead gated behind attorney review and the Utah Rules of Professional Conduct. See our Utah data-sources & methodology.
