### Most common ROI triggers found

- **Fast growth / acquisition / new branches** (fiber, restoration, landscaping roll-ups) — new fleet outruns visibility.
- **Surge & emergency dispatch** (restoration, snow, traffic control) — overtime leakage and unbilled response hours.
- **Movable high-value equipment** (excavation, crane, concrete, heavy civil) — theft exposure + unprovable jobsite hours.
- **Multi-territory service fleets** (HVAC, plumbing, roofing service) — "where's my tech" and routing waste.
- **Fuel/idle at scale** — every 250+ vehicle operator loses low-single-digit % before anyone sees it.

### Best buyer titles to target

Owner / President / COO · VP or Director of Operations · **Fleet Manager / Equipment Manager** ·
Dispatch Manager · Safety Manager · and — for national enterprises — the **regional GM / branch Ops Manager**
(that's who can pilot 50–200 units locally).

## Why the enterprise giants are scored on *branch* fit

Firms like BELFOR, Tecta America, CentiMark, BrightView, Lane Construction, and Quanta business units
are on the list, but they likely already run enterprise telematics and buy centrally. For a **50–200 unit
deal** the play is a **single regional branch beachhead** — pilot one region, prove ROI, then expand.
That reality is baked into their "GPS 365 Fit" sub-score (tab 5), which is why pure mid-market regionals
outrank them.
