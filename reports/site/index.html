Verified Market Intelligence

Weber County facts that make or break a deal

🧾 Property taxes

Effective residential rate ~0.60% of market value (≈35% below the U.S. average). Utah gives primary residences a 45% exemption — only 55% of value is taxable, already baked into that rate. On a $340–380k home: ~$2,050–$2,300/yr. Confirm the exact per-parcel rate on the tax notice.

🌊 Flood & hazard

Ogden faces Ogden/Weber River, flash-flood and Zone AO sheet-flow risk. Common zones: AE, AO, X — AE/AO generally require flood insurance. Also check Wasatch Fault liquefaction, bench landslide, and radon. Run every address through the FEMA map.

🏫 Schools

Weber District serves North/South Ogden (generally rated higher); Ogden City District serves Ogden city; charters exist (DaVinci, UTA-MES). Attendance is address-specific and boundaries change — confirm the assigned school per candidate, don't trust the listing.

📈 Market pace

Ogden ~13 DOM (fast, competitive) vs. South Ogden ~54 and North Ogden ~49 (slower = negotiation room). Below-median segment can over-appraise-gap on fixers — keep your appraisal contingency.

  • Age/condition of roof, HVAC, water heater, windows, sewer line, electrical panel?
  • Has the basement ever taken water? Were basement bedrooms/baths permitted (egress)?

Offer-strategy template (per candidate)
  • Opening offer — informed by verified comps + DOM.
  • Max recommended price — set it before you tour and hold the line.
  • Keep inspection, appraisal, and financing contingencies. In this below-median segment, the appraisal contingency matters most.
  • Concessions to request: rate buydown, closing costs, home warranty, repair credit.
  • Do not waive: inspection, appraisal, sewer scope, or major-systems repairs.
  • Walk away if: foundation/structural failure, uncurable unpermitted living space, Zone AE cost that erases the value, or comps don't support the price.
