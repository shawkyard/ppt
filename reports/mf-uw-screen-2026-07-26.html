Tier 3 · pass on thesis

The Logan, UT cluster — priced core, no lever

Four Logan-area listings share one profile, and it is exactly the one the mandate exists to avoid: sub-6% caps at $187k–$287k per unit in a tight, small market. That is stabilized, fully-priced product — a yield-and-appreciation bet, not a value-add screen. This is the "Summit Residences" pattern from the model: nothing to fix, priced to a low cap, no going-in margin.

Twin Terrace Village ($14.995M · 65u · 5.2% · $230,692/u) · Logan Area MF Portfolio ($37.0M · 159u · 5.6% · $232,704/u) · Logan Canyon Apartments ($3.0M · 16u · 5.8% · $187,500/u) · Green Creek Townhomes ($2.29M · 8u · 5.5% · $286,696/u). All pass unless a basis reset of 20%+ appears.

  • Hotels / motels (3): Carthage Motel TX, Crystal Inn St. Petersburg FL, 326 6th Ave N St. Petersburg FL.
  • Mixed-use / non-apartment dominant (8): Daytona lakefront dev, Lagrangeville NY, Lakehills TX, Jacksonville FL, Coral Gables office/MF, Dripping Springs TX, Green River UT (business+hospitality), Selma TX (vacant, hospitality/retail).

14 sub-scale — below the mandate

Real apartments, but duplex / triplex / four-plex product too small for a professional value-add business plan. Track only if a portfolio roll-up thesis emerges:

  • Houston tri-plex ($450k), New Orleans 3-plex (11.5% cap, $324.5k), Kansas City duplex ($499k)
  • RGV small MF: Mission ×2, McAllen, Alamo TX
  • Coastal FL small MF: LBTS short-term-rental (2.3% cap), Placido Place, Pompano, Lauderhill
  • Other: Alvord TX, Chicago S. Marquette ($161k), Houston Kittridge ($235k)

Notable outlier: the New Orleans 3-plex shows an 11.5% cap, but at 3 units it's below scale — a candidate only inside a neighborhood roll-up.

Bottom line. Of 51 listings, three deserve an analyst's time this week — George Commons (Vincennes), Briggs Flats (Indianapolis), and Tiffany Square (Shreveport) — each contingent on the OM and T-12.
