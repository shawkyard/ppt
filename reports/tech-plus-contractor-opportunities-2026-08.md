# Tech Plus — Non-Union Contractor Opportunity Pipeline
**Skilled workforce demand intelligence: electrical, low voltage, fiber, telecom, and data center contractors**
Prepared August 21, 2026 · Focus: non-union / open-shop / merit-shop contractors with active or imminent labor scaling needs

---

## Executive summary

The U.S. mission-critical construction market is in a structural labor deficit: ~76 new data center projects worth $88B are slated to begin by early 2026, against ~439,000 open construction positions reported in late 2025. Medium-voltage switchgear lead times of 40–60 weeks are compressing electrical scopes into shorter windows, driving overtime dependency and travel crews — exactly the conditions under which open-shop contractors buy contingent labor.

**The highest-value opportunities are pre-posting**: projects that have been awarded, permitted, or announced where craft recruiting has not yet hit the job boards. Outreach that lands 60–120 days before a contractor's internal recruiting ramp is how a staffing partner gets written into the project labor plan instead of competing on rate cards later.

### 1. IES Communications / IES Holdings — Score 5.0
- **Signal detected:** Backlog $4.5B as of June 30, 2026 — **up 91%** since Sept 2025. Communications segment revenue +51% YoY on data center demand. The company's own 10-Q states that **"availability of labor and capacity could constrain the rate at which we are able to grow"** in data centers — a public admission of the exact problem Tech Plus solves.
- **Project/expansion:** Hyperscale data center cabling and commissioning across AZ, TX, VA and national footprint; 150+ data center technician requisitions typically open.
- **Estimated workforce demand:** Hundreds of data center technicians, structured cabling techs, and commissioning support across FY2026–2027 to execute backlog.
- **Union status:** Non-union.
- **Decision-makers (Apollo-verified):** Head of Talent Acquisition (Delina P.), VP of National Operations (Katherine F.), VP of Business Operations (David C., Alex B.). Emails/direct dials available via Apollo enrichment.
- **Outreach angle:** Quote their own disclosure back to them — backlog is up 91% and labor availability is the stated constraint on converting it to revenue. Position Tech Plus as the variable-capacity layer that lets them say yes to the next award without waiting on requisition cycles.
