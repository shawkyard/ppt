Local Search & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Ogden Coffee House located?

Ogden Coffee House is located at 2401 Historic 25th Street in downtown Ogden, Utah, in the heart of the historic 25th Street district.

Does Ogden Coffee House serve breakfast all day?

Ogden Coffee House serves a full breakfast menu from open until 11 AM on weekdays and until noon on weekends, alongside espresso and pastries available all day.

Can I order ahead from Ogden Coffee House?

Guests can call ahead to place a pickup order, and group or office coffee orders can be arranged directly with the shop.

Does Ogden Coffee House have decaf or non-dairy options?

Yes. Ogden Coffee House offers decaf espresso and oat, almond, and soy milk alternatives for any drink on the menu.
