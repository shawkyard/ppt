The problem

One Cuisine Never Fits the Whole Group

The bigger the group, the more the menu matters. A burger place loses the people avoiding it; a single-cuisine spot loses the ones who had it yesterday; and any restaurant without halal options quietly excludes whoever eats halal — usually leaving them with a side salad and a polite smile. Add the logistics of a large order on top, and the organizer ends up doing real work just to get everyone fed. Eating together is supposed to build the team, not test it.

Our answer

Two Cuisines, One Order, Nobody Left Out

Fajita Grill runs a Mexican kitchen and a Mediterranean kitchen under one roof — and both are halal. That single fact dissolves most group-meal problems: the taco people, the shawarma people and the halal eaters all order off the same menu, on the same ticket. Call ahead for large orders and we'll have everything timed and packed; for full-on events, our catering service takes it from there.

  • Mexican and Mediterranean on one menu — every vote counts
  • Halal in both kitchens, so no one is quietly excluded
  • Fajitas, big burritos and nachos that are made for sharing
  • Call-ahead large orders, packed and ready on time
  • Catering for special events when the group becomes a crowd

Group FAQ

Questions Group Organizers Ask Us

How far ahead should I call for a big order?

Just call us at (801) 996-3715 as soon as you know — tell us the headcount and the pickup time, and we'll work out what's doable together. More notice always helps, but start with a call either way.

Can a group dine in at the restaurant?

Yes — groups are welcome in our Midvale dining room. For larger parties, call ahead and we'll help make sure there's room set up for everyone when you arrive.

When should I use catering instead of a large takeout order?

Rule of thumb: if it's a meal, a big takeout order works great; if it's an event — an office party, a celebration, a gathering with a set time and place — our catering service is built for it. Not sure? Call and we'll point you the right way.

Can one order really cover mixed dietary needs?

That's our specialty: two cuisines and halal in both kitchens means meat eaters, halal eaters and the hard-to-please can all order off the same menu. See our halal page for the details.

Can a group order be delivered?

We deliver through DoorDash — check your delivery address in the app. For large orders, a call-ahead pickup is often the smoothest option, and we'll have everything bagged by group or by person if you ask.
