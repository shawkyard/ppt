The problem

Most Fajitas Show Up Tired

Fajitas are supposed to be an event — vegetables still cooking when they reach the table, fillings generous enough to build every bite the way you want. Too often, what arrives is the opposite: a lukewarm skillet, thin fillings stretched across a big plate, and sides sold separately. When a restaurant treats fajitas as just another line on the menu, you can taste it. When a restaurant is named after them, it can't afford to.

Our answer

A Fajita Plate That's Actually Complete

Every fajita order at Fajita Grill is made to order: your choice of steak, chicken, shrimp or mix, grilled with chopped bell peppers, onion, tomato and melted cheese, then served with rice, beans, a small salad with sour cream, and handmade tortillas. That's the whole plate, as written on our menu — not a base price with the sides bolted on.

  • Made fresh when you order — not held under a lamp
  • Bell peppers, onions & tomatoes cooked right in
  • Topped with a small salad, sour cream and cheese
  • Rice and beans included with the plate
  • From a halal kitchen, like everything else we serve

Fajita FAQ

Questions Fajita Fans Ask Us

What comes with a fajita plate?

Choose steak, chicken, shrimp or the mix — grilled with chopped bell peppers, onion, tomato and melted cheese, served with rice, beans, a small salad with sour cream, and handmade tortillas. Chips and salsa come on the side with every order.

Can I get fajitas for takeout?

Yes — order ahead online or call (801) 996-3715 and we'll have your fajitas ready for pickup. We cook them fresh when your order comes in.

Can you handle fajitas for a group?

Absolutely. Bring your group to dine in, call ahead for a larger takeout order, or — for offices and events — see our catering page.

Are the fajitas halal?

Yes. Fajita Grill serves halal in both kitchens, so our fajitas — and the rest of the menu — come from a halal kitchen. More on our halal dining page.

Where do I find you?
