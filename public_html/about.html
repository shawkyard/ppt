How we cook

Two Kitchens, One Standard

Most restaurants pick a lane. We picked two — and held both to the same rule: cook it the homemade way, with high-quality ingredients and vibrant spices, and make it halal without exception.

That means the Mexican kitchen turning out fajitas, street tacos, burritos, quesadillas, tortas and sopes works to the same standard as the Mediterranean kitchen serving shawarma, starters, entrees and hummus platters. Different flavors, same care.

  • Homemade cooking — plates made fresh, not pulled from a warmer
  • High-quality ingredients and vibrant spices in every dish
  • Halal in both kitchens — Mexican and Mediterranean alike
  • Breakfast through dinner, from 9 AM every day

A new chapter

Our New Midvale Home
