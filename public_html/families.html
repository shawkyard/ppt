The problem

One Restaurant Rarely Fits a Whole Family

Most restaurants do one thing. That's fine for a couple — but for a family, it means somebody always compromises. The kids' favorite spot bores the parents; the parents' pick gets a groan from the back seat; and if your family eats halal, the list of places where everyone can order freely gets shorter still. So family dinner out either becomes a debate, or it becomes the same place every single time. Dinner together should be the easy part of the week, not another decision to manage.

Our answer

Two Cuisines, One Table — and a Kids Menu Too

Fajita Grill puts Mexican and Mediterranean cooking under one roof in our new Midvale home on State Street. Street tacos, burritos, quesadillas and sopes from one kitchen; shawarma, Mediterranean entrees and starters from the other; a dedicated Kids menu and desserts for the finish — all halal, all on the same ticket. Eat in our dining room, or take it home for family night on the couch.

Family FAQ

Questions Parents Ask Us

Do you have a kids menu?

Yes — the menu has a dedicated Kids section, alongside desserts and drinks, so younger eaters get their own picks. See the full menu.

Do you have high chairs or seating for larger families?

Give us a quick call at (801) 996-3715 before you come and we'll tell you exactly what we have and help set up the right table for your group.

Can we get takeout for a family night at home?

Absolutely. Order online or call ahead and pick it all up in one trip — or have it delivered via DoorDash and skip the drive entirely.

Are you open on weekends?

Yes. We're open Saturday 9 AM–9 PM and Sunday 9 AM–5 PM, so weekend breakfast, lunch and an early Sunday dinner are all on the table.

Is everything halal, even the kids meals?

Yes — both of our kitchens are halal, so every item on the menu, Kids section included, is halal. More on that on our halal dining page.
