The problem

Good Breakfast Is the Hardest Meal to Find

Dinner spots are everywhere; breakfast is where the options thin out. Around the valley, a hot morning meal too often means fast food eaten in the car — and if you eat halal, the breakfast map shrinks to almost nothing. Cereal at home wins by default, not by choice. The first meal of the day shouldn't be the one you've given up on; it should be the one that makes the rest of the day easier.

Our answer

Breakfast Burritos and Plates, Off the Grill From 9 AM

Our menu opens the day with two dedicated breakfast categories: Breakfast Burritos and Breakfast Plates, cooked to order on the same grill that sizzles our fajitas. Breakfast FAQ

Questions Early Birds Ask Us

What time does breakfast start?

Breakfast is served from open — 9 AM every day of the week.

How late can I order breakfast?

If you're coming later in the day and have your heart set on a breakfast burrito or plate, give us a quick call at (801) 996-3715 to confirm the cutoff before you head over.

Do you serve breakfast on weekends?

Yes — we open at 9 AM on Saturday and Sunday too, so weekend breakfast is always on. (Sundays we close at 5 PM.)

Can I pick up breakfast for a whole crew?

Happily. Call ahead or order online and we'll have burritos and plates bagged and ready for the job site, the office or the carpool. For bigger events, see our catering page.

Is breakfast halal?

Yes — both of our kitchens are halal, breakfast included. More on our halal dining page.
