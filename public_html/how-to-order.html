Ordering FAQ

Quick Answers Before You Order

What's the fastest way to get takeout?

Call ahead at (801) 996-3715 or order on our online ordering site — either way, your food is cooking before you leave the house.

How does delivery work?

We deliver through DoorDash — look for "Fajita grill to go". Delivery hours, zones and fees are set within the DoorDash app. You can also order at our online ordering site.

Do I need a reservation to dine in?

No — just walk in and order at the counter. If you're bringing a larger group and want to check ahead, call us at (801) 996-3715.

How far ahead should I ask about catering?

The earlier the better — it gives us time to plan your menu. Start on the catering page or call (801) 996-3715 to talk through your event.
