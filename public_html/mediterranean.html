The problem

Good Mediterranean Food Shouldn't Be a Road Trip

In the Salt Lake Valley, finding Mediterranean food done with care often means a long drive — or settling for a restaurant where shawarma is one lonely line at the bottom of someone else's menu. An afterthought menu produces afterthought food. And when the rest of your group isn't in the mood for Mediterranean at all, the negotiation starts before you've even left the house. You shouldn't have to choose between the food you're craving and a place everyone agrees on.

Our answer

A Real Mediterranean Menu — Not a Footnote

Our Mediterranean kitchen has its own sections on the menu: Mediterranean Starters and Mediterranean Entrees, with shawarma and hummus platters at the heart of it. It's halal, like everything we serve. And because our Mexican kitchen runs right beside it, the rest of your table can order fajitas, street tacos or quesadillas in the same trip.

Mediterranean FAQ

Questions Mediterranean Diners Ask Us

What's on the Mediterranean menu?

The Mediterranean side of our menu has its own Starters and Entrees sections, including shawarma and hummus platters. For the full item list and live prices, see the menu or order online.

Is the Mediterranean food halal?

Yes — and so is everything else. Fajita Grill serves halal in both kitchens, Mediterranean and Mexican alike. More on our halal dining page.

Can I mix Mexican and Mediterranean in one order?

Yes, and people do it all the time. Shawarma for you, street tacos for them, a hummus platter for the table — it all comes from the same counter in one order.

Can you cater a Mediterranean spread?

Yes — we cater special events, and Mediterranean starters, entrees and hummus platters travel well for groups. Start on our catering page or call (801) 996-3715.
