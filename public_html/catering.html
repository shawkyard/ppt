The problem

Feeding a Mixed Group Is the Hardest Job in the Room

Every planner knows the math: the bigger the group, the more dietary needs, preferences and strong opinions you have to satisfy at once. Halal guests too often get an apologetic side salad. One-cuisine spreads wear thin by the third event. And a vendor who's hard to reach turns a catering order into a week of anxiety. The food should be the easy part of your event — not the variable you lose sleep over.

Our answer

Two Kitchens, One Vendor, Halal Throughout

Fajita Grill caters special events with both of our kitchens. The Mexican side brings fajitas, street tacos, burritos and quesadillas; the Mediterranean side brings starters, entrees, shawarma and hummus platters. Everything is halal, so the guests who usually have to ask careful questions can simply eat. And because we're a local restaurant on State Street, the person planning your menu is the same kitchen cooking it.

  • Halal in both kitchens — every guest covered by default
  • Mexican and Mediterranean from a single vendor
  • Menus planned with you, around your group and event
  • Cooked fresh by the same kitchens that serve our dining room
  • A real phone number that reaches real people: (801) 996-3715

Catering FAQ

Questions Event Planners Ask Us

How far in advance should I book?

The earlier the better, but don't rule yourself out — call (801) 996-3715 with your date and we'll work with you on what's possible.

Is there a minimum guest count?

Tell us your size and we'll figure out the right fit. Smaller gathering? Dining in at our Midvale location or a large takeout order may suit you better — we'll say so honestly.

Is the catering halal?

Yes — everything comes from the same two halal kitchens, Mexican and Mediterranean alike. More on our halal dining page.

Do you deliver the catering to my venue?

Logistics depend on your event — location, timing and setup. Call us at (801) 996-3715 and we'll discuss what works for your venue when we plan the menu.

Can you mix Mexican and Mediterranean in one spread?

That's our favorite kind of order. Fajitas and street tacos on one table, shawarma and hummus platters on the next — one vendor, one delivery, everyone fed.
