Seven Simple Steps

From “who do you want?” to “more sales”

1️⃣

Define the customers you actually want

ICP means ideal customer profile — the type of customer your business is built to serve best. We help you pin down exactly who that is.

2️⃣

Create a real-value offer

A meaningful offer those customers would care about — like a free hour, a $100 assessment, or a facility walkthrough. Real value, not a fake coupon.

3️⃣

Build a premium mini-site inside the network

A beautiful, standardized 5-page sales site. Your brand, your colors, your offer — with a consistent premium layout.

4️⃣

Add audience pages for each customer type

People buy the same service for different reasons. Each audience page speaks directly to one buyer — their problem, their world, their reason to buy.

5️⃣

Use those pages for SEO, campaigns & outreach

Your pages become landing pages for search, email, and outbound. Demand comes to you (farming) and you can go get it (hunting).

6️⃣

Use LeadGen tools to find better-fit companies

Research the right companies and likely decision-makers, then generate personalized outreach drafts you approve before sending.

7️⃣

Turn better-fit leads into more sales

One great lead beats ten bad ones. Better fit means longer, more profitable, recurring relationships — the customers who buy, stay, and succeed with you.
