Lunch FAQ

Questions the Lunch Crowd Asks Us

How do I order ahead for pickup?

Order online through our ordering page at fajitagrillut.com, or call (801) 996-3715 and we'll have it ready when you arrive.

What are your lunch hours?

We're open Mon–Sat 9 AM–9 PM and Sunday 9 AM–5 PM, so the full lunch window is covered every day of the week. If you have a tight pickup time, call ahead and we'll work with you.

Can you handle a group order for the office?

Yes — for a handful of coworkers, see our groups and team meals page; for meetings and events, our catering page covers the bigger spreads. A quick call ahead helps us time a large order to your break.

Do you deliver to offices?

We deliver via DoorDash — enter your office address in the app to confirm availability, and lunch comes to the building instead of you coming to us.

Is the lunch menu halal?

Everything is — both of our kitchens are halal, lunch included. Details on our halal dining page.
