Before You Head Over

Do you take reservations?

It's a walk-in, order-at-the-counter kind of place — but if you want to check seating before you come, call us at (801) 996-3715 and we'll sort you out.

Can we bring a group?

Absolutely — and a call ahead helps us time everything together. See the groups & team meals page for how to make a group visit easy.

First time — what should I order?

We wrote a whole guide for exactly this: the first-order field guide. Short version: fajitas if you want the namesake, hummus platter and shawarma if you lean Mediterranean — or mix both kitchens in one order.
