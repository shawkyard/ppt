Responsible Outreach

Powerful, but responsible by design

This is not spam automation. We build in guardrails so your outreach stays professional and compliant.

✅ What LeadGen does

  • Research prospects & build lead lists
  • Identify likely decision-maker roles
  • Create personalized outreach drafts
  • Let you approve every message before sending
  • Track campaigns & export results
  • Manage compliance & do-not-contact settings

🚫 What LeadGen never does

  • No “scrape LinkedIn”
  • No auto-sending LinkedIn messages — drafts only
  • No unlimited blast messaging
  • No SMS unless the recipient opts in
  • No misrepresenting your identity

Our responsible-outreach rules

  • Use accurate sender identity
  • Use honest subject lines
  • Include opt-out language for commercial email
  • Maintain do-not-contact lists
  • LinkedIn messages are draft-only
  • SMS is opt-in only
  • Review messages before sending
  • Respect platform rules
