Your First-Order Field Guide

Two kitchens, one menu, and you've got one stomach — choices must be made. Here's an honest guide to navigating your first Fajita Grill order, whichever direction your appetite leans.

Open the Menu Skip Ahead & Order

One rule before we start: everything below is halal — both kitchens, no asterisks.

The obvious answer

If You're Coming for the Namesake

We're called Fajita Grill for a reason. The fajitas are cooked with bell peppers, onions & tomatoes, topped with a small salad, sour cream and cheese, and served with rice and beans — a complete plate, no sides required.

If it's your first visit and you want the dish that defines the house, this is it. Decision made.

The other kitchen

If You're Team Mediterranean

The Mediterranean side of the house runs from a starter menu — hummus platters included — through full entrees, shawarma among them, all seasoned with the vibrant spices we cook with everywhere. Start with a hummus platter for the table, then pick an entree. You won't go wrong.

Before noon

If It's Morning

We open at 9 AM, and breakfast opens with us: breakfast burritos when you're eating on the move, breakfast plates when you've got time to sit. Either way, it's the same homemade, halal kitchen behind it.

Small appetites

Feeding Kids

There's a dedicated kids menu, so the little ones get their own order instead of picking at yours — and desserts and drinks to close the deal. Quesadillas from the Mexican kitchen are a reliable crowd-pleaser for younger diners, too.

How Family Visits Work
The secret option

Can't Decide? Don't.

Here's the move regulars figure out fast: you don't have to choose a kitchen. Street tacos and a hummus platter in the same order. A fajita plate for you, shawarma for whoever you're with. Both kitchens, one counter, one bag. That's the whole point of the place.
