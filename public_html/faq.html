The food

Food & Halal

Is everything on the menu halal?

Yes — Fajita Grill serves halal in both kitchens, Mexican and Mediterranean. You don't have to pick from a special section or quiz the staff item by item. More on our halal dining page.

What comes with the fajitas?

Our fajitas are cooked with bell peppers, onions & tomatoes, topped with a small salad, sour cream and cheese, and served with rice and beans. See the fajitas page for more.

Do you have vegetarian-friendly options?

Our Mediterranean starters include hummus platters, and there are veggie-friendly options across the menu — but choices vary, so ask our team when you order and we'll point you to what fits. Call (801) 996-3715 if you'd like to check before visiting.

Is there a kids menu?

Yes — we have a dedicated kids menu, plus desserts and drinks. See the family dinners page for how a family visit works.

What does "two kitchens" actually mean?

One restaurant, two cuisines: a Mexican kitchen (fajitas, street tacos, burritos, quesadillas, tortas, sopes, nachos, plates) and a Mediterranean kitchen (shawarma, starters, entrees, hummus platters). You can mix both in one order. Browse the full menu.

The visit

Visiting Us

Where are you located?

We're at 7680 S State St, Midvale, UT 84047 — and yes, that's a new address. We just moved, so if you knew us from before, come find us at the new place. Plan your visit here.

The order

Ordering

Can I order online?

Yes — our online ordering site is www.fajitagrillut.com, with live prices for pickup and delivery. Or start on our order page.

Do you deliver?

Yes, through DoorDash — find us as "Fajita grill to go" (4.7 stars from 100+ customers). Delivery hours and fees are set in the DoorDash app. Open our DoorDash store.

Can I call ahead for takeout?

Please do — call (801) 996-3715 and we'll have your order ready at the counter when you arrive.

Do you cater events?

Yes — we cater special events, from offices to parties and gatherings, all from the same halal kitchens. Start on the catering page or call to talk through your event.

We're a big group — can you handle a large order?

Yes. For large group orders, the best move is to call ahead at (801) 996-3715 so we can time everything together. See the groups & team meals page for tips.
