The problem

Delivery Shouldn't Feel Like Roulette

Everyone knows the gamble: you pick something from an app, hope it travels well, and hope the kitchen cooked it when the driver was close — not half an hour earlier. For halal diners, the odds are worse, because the apps offer slim pickings and it's hard to verify what's actually halal from a listing. Staying in shouldn't mean settling. It should mean the same food you'd choose in person, arriving the way it left the kitchen.

Delivery & Takeout FAQ

Questions Before You Order In

How do I order pickup?

Order online at fajitagrillut.com, or call (801) 996-3715 and we'll have it ready at 7680 S State St in Midvale.

Do you deliver to my area?

Delivery coverage is set by the DoorDash app based on your address. Enter your address on our DoorDash store page and it will tell you right away.

What are the delivery hours?

Per the app, delivery is listed from 9:30 AM to 8:40 PM — but DoorDash sets and updates those hours, so check the app for the current window. Our store hours are Mon–Sat 9 AM–9 PM and Sun 9 AM–5 PM.

Can I place a big delivery order for a group?

For larger orders, the best move is to call us at (801) 996-3715 so we can plan timing. For offices and events, our catering page is the right starting point.

Is the delivery food halal too?

Yes. It's the same two halal kitchens whether you dine in, pick up or get it delivered. Details on our halal dining page.
