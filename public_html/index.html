The Menu

Two Cuisines, One Homemade Standard

Everything is cooked with high-quality ingredients and vibrant spices — and it's all halal, in both kitchens.

🔥 Sizzling Fajitas

Steak, chicken, shrimp or mix — grilled with peppers, onion, tomato and melted cheese, served with rice, beans, salad and handmade tortillas.

🌮 Tacos, Birria & More

Street tacos, slow-cooked birria with consomé, burritos, quesadillas, enchiladas, tortas, sopes and nachos — the homemade Mexican lineup.
