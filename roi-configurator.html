The math behind the numbers

No black box. Here's exactly how the configurator gets to your ROI.

  • ✓Baseline revenue = customers × visits/month × average order value — your business as it is today.
  • ✓Members = customers × enrollment rate. Only members drive the lift.
  • ✓Incremental revenue comes from two levers: members visiting more often (frequency lift) and spending more each visit (average-check lift).
  • ✓Costs = reward redemptions (a % of member sales) ＋ your flat monthly platform fee.
  • ✓Net gain = incremental revenue − costs. ROI = net gain ÷ total program cost. Conservative by design — it credits loyalty only for the extra activity it creates.
