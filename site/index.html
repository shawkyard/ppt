Target profile

Know what you're hunting

An underperforming C property in a B area in a strong or emerging market, with fixable upside — and a realistic capex path.

▲ We hunt this

  • + Under-market rents with a real gap to close
  • + Vacancy upside & sloppy operations to tighten
  • + Dated units — fixable, not luxury
  • + Weak or uncaptured other income (RUBS, parking, pet, laundry)
  • + Stale listing, negotiable price, absentee owner
  • + Strong or emerging market with rent-growth tailwind

▼ We pass on this

  • – Luxury / stabilized / fully-renovated product
  • – Bad locations that never recover
  • – Deals that only pencil off the broker pro forma
  • – No rent upside — nothing to add
  • – Appreciation-only bets with no operating lever
  • – Soft exit caps with no margin of safety on basis

The scoring, in the open

Market gate — 0–100

Job growth (15) · population growth (12) · rent growth (12) · wage support (10) · employer anchors (10) · new-supply risk (10, inverted) · affordability (8) · crime/safety risk (8, inverted) · RE Indicator (10) · infrastructure (5).

80–100 Approved · 60–79 Watchlist · <60 Reject

Property scratch — 0–100

Market strength (20) · submarket quality (15) · condition / value-add fit (15) · rent upside (15) · vacancy & ops upside (10) · capex feasibility (10) · debt / strike factor (10) · broker-optimism risk (5, inverted). Market, rent and vacancy upside are auto-derived from the data.

85+ Strong · 70+ Request OM · 55+ Watchlist · 40+ Pass unless price drops · <40 Pass

Deal math

Price/unit · monthly & annual rent upside · current & stabilized gross rent, NOI and cap rate · stabilized value at the exit cap · total project cost · value created.
