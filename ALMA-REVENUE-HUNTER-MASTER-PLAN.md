# Deliverable 1 — Executive Brand Brief

**What the business is.** Prestige Sales Experts is a done-for-you revenue company. Its customer-facing
product line is **Alma Revenue Hunter**: an intelligence system plus a coordinated fleet of specialist
agents that finds revenue opportunities, builds the web presence that attracts buyers, and installs the
systems that convert them.

**What Alma is.** One intelligence system (not 100 disconnected bots) that studies a business, learns its
ideal buyers, detects opportunity signals, finds matching companies and decision-makers, explains *why an
opportunity exists now*, and recommends the next action. Delivered done-for-you today; DIY later.

**Hunt / Farm / Grow.**
- **HUNT** — agents actively find buyers, signals, decision-makers, projects, unbilled work, reactivation and market opportunities. *Symbol: photorealistic great white shark.* Lead category.
- **FARM** — websites, audience/industry pages, SEO/AI-search, lead capture that turn the business into a magnet for the right buyers. *Symbol: giant commercial electromagnet (not agriculture).*
- **GROW** — automation, conversion, reactivation, loyalty, retention, revenue recovery that turn demand into repeatable momentum. *Symbol: cinematic rocket ignition.*

**Who the customer is.** Owners and revenue leaders of small–mid businesses across ~100 industries
(trades, IT/MSP, real estate, legal, healthcare, home services, professional services, etc.) who have a
website and maybe a CRM but no fleet actively hunting revenue.

**Why we're different (the 5-second thesis).** *Your website waits. Alma hunts.* Everyone has a website;
almost nobody has intelligent agents out finding opportunities before competitors see them.

**Primary offer:** Start a Hunting Expedition (done-for-you). **Secondary:** Farm (web/audience pages),
Grow (revenue systems), and combined Hunt+Farm / full Hunt·Farm·Grow systems.

**What the visitor should do:** Start a Hunting Expedition, or use the guided recommendation tool.

# Deliverable 13 — Technical Architecture

**Principle:** Manus builds; **GitHub + Cloudflare are the permanent system.** Site must keep working if Manus is paused/canceled.

- **Source of truth:** GitHub (this repo pattern), auto-deploy on push.
- **Hosting:** Cloudflare Pages (production + staging environments).
- **Backend:** Cloudflare Workers for secure functions (Stripe webhooks, intake handling, guided-tool logic).
- **Data:** Cloudflare **D1** for orders/intake/customer records; **R2** for uploaded assets/reports; **KV** for config/session; **Queues** if async fulfillment; **Turnstile** for form-spam.
- **Payments:** Stripe server-side; secrets in Worker env vars only; webhook-driven order status.
- **Content:** structured content (agents/industries/services/packages/case-studies/articles/FAQs/videos/CTAs) so new agent/industry pages are data, not rebuilds — but not mechanically generated.
- **Ops:** documented build command + output dir, DNS/domain docs, error logging, analytics, backups/export, structured data, sitemap, performance monitoring.
