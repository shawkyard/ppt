# Demo data — all illustrative, labeled

Every figure and company in this prototype is **illustrative demo material**. None of it is a
verified actual, a real competitive ranking, or a real financial model output. This file lists it
all in one place so a reviewer can confirm nothing masquerades as truth. This mirrors Alma's own
core rule: **estimated data never renders in the "Actual" style.**

## Financial engine — illustrative scenario values
Derived from a demo brand (Revenue $5.13B ÷ AOV $98 ÷ Frequency 2.4×). Scenario outputs:

| Scenario | Net contribution | Benefit-Cost Ratio | Net ROI | Payback |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conservative | +$4.2M | 2.1× | 112% | 14 mo |
| Expected | +$7.1M | 3.0× | 204% | 9 mo |
| High | +$12.0M | 4.4× | 341% | 6 mo |

These illustrate the **intended behavior** ("attractive even under conservative assumptions"),
not the real formula registry. The point of the design is the *band*, not a false-precise point number.

## Scores shown (illustrative)

- These are hard-coded to demonstrate the "five separate, drillable scores" principle.

## Provenance labels used in the UI
- `ACTUAL` (solid emerald) — e.g. Annual Revenue "from SEC 10-K"
- `~ESTIMATED` (dashed amber) — e.g. Enrollment Rate "category prior"
- `HYBRID` (indigo) — e.g. AOV "derived + benchmark"
- `MISSING` (dotted grey) — e.g. Current Loyalty Vendor "no evidence found"
- Web reach is labeled **Digital Demand signal**, never converted to audited revenue.

## Pricing & budget figures
All pricing ($99–199, $299–499, $1,000/mo, credit packs) and the ~$2,000/mo operating budget
are **proposed concepts**, explicitly labeled as such in the UI — not finalized pricing or guarantees.
